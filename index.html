What Are We Writing at This Level?

At grades 11–12 level, writing means more than just covering the topic. You need to develop your ideas, use a rich variety of language, and show the examiner that you can write with clarity, structure, and depth — in at least 150 words. בכיתות י"א–י"ב, כתיבה טובה היא הרבה יותר מסתם כיסוי הנושא. צריך לפתח רעיונות, להשתמש במגוון שפה עשירה, ולהראות בוחן שאתם יכולים לכתוב בבהירות, מבנה ועומק — בלפחות 150 מילים.

🖼️ Descriptive Text

Create a vivid, multi-layered portrait — of a place, person, experience, or phenomenon. Use precise vocabulary, sensory language, and varied sentence structures. תאור מרובד ועשיר — של מקום, אדם, חוויה, או תופעה. שפה מדויקת, חושנית, ומשפטים מגוונים.

💬 Argumentative Text

Present a well-reasoned position. Acknowledge the opposing view, support your arguments with examples or evidence, and conclude with conviction. הצגת עמדה מנומקת. הכרה בדעה הנגדית, תמיכה בטיעונים עם דוגמאות, וסיכום משכנע.
📏 The 150+ Word Standard: Your writing should be at least 150 words. A well-developed text will typically reach 180–220 words. Think in paragraphs, not just sentences — each paragraph has a role to play. הכתיבה שלכם צריכה להיות לפחות 150 מילים. טקסט מפותח יגיע בדרך כלל ל-180–220 מילים. חשבו בפסקאות, לא רק במשפטים — לכל פסקה תפקיד.
🏗️
The Multi-Paragraph Essay Structure

A 150+ word essay needs more than one paragraph. Think of it as an expanded sandwich: חיבור של 150+ מילים צריך יותר מפסקה אחת. חשבו על זה כסנדוויץ׳ מורחב:

🍞 Introduction — Opening Paragraph Hook the reader + introduce the topic + state your main idea (thesis) פתיחה מושכת + הצגת הנושא + הצהרת הרעיון המרכזי
🥬 Body Paragraph 1 — First Main Point Topic sentence → Explanation → Example / Evidence → Link back משפט נושא → הסבר → דוגמה/עדות → חיבור חזרה לנושא
🥩 Body Paragraph 2 — Second Main Point Topic sentence → Explanation → Example / Evidence → Link back משפט נושא → הסבר → דוגמה/עדות → חיבור חזרה לנושא
🧀 Counter-argument / Extra Point Recommended Acknowledge the other side — then refute or qualify it הכירו בצד השני — ואז הפריכו או הסייגו
🫐 Body Paragraph 3 — Further Development Add a third point, deepen your argument, or widen the scope נקודה שלישית, העמקת הטיעון, או הרחבת ההיקף
🍞 Conclusion — Closing Paragraph Summarise + restate your position + final thought or call to action סיכום + חזרה על עמדתכם + מחשבה אחרונה או קריאה לפעולה
⚡ Opening Paragraph — Example
The question of whether social media has transformed human connection for better or for worse has become one of the defining debates of our time. Although many people believe that platforms such as Instagram and WhatsApp have brought us closer together, one must always remember that there is usually more than one side to this issue. In this essay, I will argue that while social media offers undeniable benefits, its long-term impact on genuine human relationships deserves serious concern.

🟡 Topic sentence = orange highlight | 🟡 Thesis = orange highlight | Middle = development

Descriptive Writing — Painting with Precision

Advanced descriptive writing goes beyond listing details. You must create an atmosphere, choose words with care, and take the reader on a journey. Use figurative language, varied sentence length, and all five senses. כתיבה תיאורית ברמה גבוהה עוברת מעבר לרישום פרטים. אתם צריכים ליצור אווירה, לבחור מילים בקפידה, ולקחת את הקורא למסע. שפה פיגורטיבית, אורכי משפטים מגוונים, וחמשת החושים.

🎭 Atmosphere & Mood

Don't just describe — feel the place. Is it melancholy? Energetic? Unsettling? Every detail should support the mood.
אל רק תתארו — חושו את המקום. האם הוא עצוב? נמרץ? מטריד? כל פרט צריך לתמוך באווירה.

🖌️ Figurative Language

Simile: "like a…" / "as … as …"
Metaphor: "The city was a machine…"
Personification: "The wind whispered…"
השוואה / מטאפורה / אנשת דומם — שפה ציורית

📐 Sentence Variety

Mix short punchy sentences with longer, flowing ones. Start sentences differently — not always with "The" or "I".
שלבו משפטים קצרים עם ארוכים. התחילו משפטים בצורות שונות.

🔍 Precise Vocabulary

Replace: said → murmured / declared
walked → strode / wandered
big → vast / overwhelming
בחרו מילים מדויקות ועשירות יותר

How to Build a 150+ Word Descriptive Text
🍞 Introduction — Set the Scene ▼

Your opening paragraph should draw the reader in immediately. Avoid starting with "I" — use one of these powerful openers from the official phrase bank: הפסקה הפותחת צריכה למשוך את הקורא מיד. הימנעו מהתחלה עם "I" — השתמשו באחד מהפותחים החזקים האלה:

The phenomenon concerning... Recently, we have become increasingly aware of... There are some factors which must be taken into account... When discussing the issue of... It is a commonly held belief that...
✅ Strong Descriptive Opening
There are some factors which must be taken into account when trying to understand the particular magic of the old city at dawn. Long before the shopkeepers arrive, the narrow alleys belong entirely to the morning light — pale gold and uncommonly still.
🥬🥩 Body Paragraphs — Layers of Detail ▼

Each body paragraph should open with a topic sentence, develop with detail and figurative language, and link forward. Use middle-paragraph connectors: כל פסקת גוף צריכה לפתוח עם משפט נושא, להתפתח עם פרטים ושפה ציורית, ולקשר קדימה. השתמשו במחברים לפסקות אמצע:

🟢 Opening a body paragraph

First and foremost, it is important to state that…
To begin with, it is worth mentioning that…
First, it is essential to remember that… פתיחת פסקת גוף ראשונה

🔵 Adding another paragraph

Another point that should be taken into consideration is…
A further dimension of this experience is…
It is a well-known fact that… פתיחת פסקת גוף נוספת
✅ Body Paragraph — Descriptive
First and foremost, it is important to state that the market's power lies not in any single detail, but in the overwhelming accumulation of them. The stalls are piled with vegetables in every shade of orange and green, their colours almost theatrical under the fluorescent lights. The air is thick with competing scents — cumin, fresh bread, something sweet and slightly burned. At the same time, the noise is relentless: vendors calling out, trolleys rattling over uneven cobblestones, a radio somewhere playing a song nobody seems to know.
🍞 Conclusion — Leave a Lasting Impression ▼

End with a paragraph that completes the circle — echo something from your opening, and leave the reader with a final image or reflection. Use concluding connectors: סיימו בפסקה שמשלימה את המעגל — חזרו לפתיחה, והשאירו בקורא דימוי או הרהור אחרון. השתמשו במחברי סיכום:

To conclude, it is necessary to remember that... Considering everything I have just written... All in all... It is about time people realized that... In conclusion, bearing this in mind...
✅ Full Descriptive Text (~175 words)
There are some factors which must be taken into account when trying to understand the particular magic of the old city market at dawn. Long before the shopkeepers arrive, the narrow alleys belong entirely to the morning light — pale gold and uncommonly still.

First and foremost, it is important to state that the market's power lies not in any single detail, but in the overwhelming accumulation of them. The stalls are piled with vegetables in every shade of orange and green. The air is thick with competing scents — cumin, fresh bread, something sweet and slightly burned. At the same time, the noise is relentless: vendors calling out, trolleys rattling, a radio playing a song nobody seems to know.

Considering everything I have just mentioned, it becomes clear that the market is far more than a place to buy and sell — it is the living memory of the city itself.

Argumentative Writing — Persuade with Evidence

At this level, a strong argument does not only state an opinion — it anticipates objections, uses research and evidence, and guides the reader to agree with you through logic and passion combined. ברמה זו, טיעון חזק לא רק מציג דעה — הוא מצפה להתנגדויות, משתמש במחקר וראיות, ומוביל את הקורא להסכים איתך דרך היגיון ותשוקה כאחד.

The Argumentative Essay Structure
Intro (hook + context + thesis) → Body 1 (first argument + evidence) → Body 2 (second argument + example) → Counter-argument (acknowledge + refute) → Conclusion (restate + call to action)
📝
How to Write a 150+ Word Argumentative Essay
🍞 Introduction — Hook, Context, Thesis ▼

Open by naming the issue, providing brief context, and ending the paragraph with a clear thesis statement. Use these academic openers: פתחו בשמות הסוגיה, הקשר קצר, וסיימו את הפסקה עם הצהרת תזה ברורה:

Although many people believe that..., it is, in my opinion, a misconception. When discussing the issue of... I firmly believe that... ... is a controversial issue. Some claim that... while others believe... The issue of ... is a source of controversy in our society. Nowadays many people are concerned about the problem of... It is a commonly held belief that... In this essay, I will point out...
✅ Academic Opening Paragraph
Although many people believe that social media has made us more connected, it is, in my opinion, a misconception. The question of how technology shapes human relationships has fascinated researchers and worried parents in equal measure. There is no doubt that platforms such as Instagram and WhatsApp offer real conveniences — yet the cost to authentic communication may be higher than we are willing to admit. In this essay, I will point out arguments which support my view that excessive social media use is damaging our ability to form meaningful relationships.
🥬🥩 Body Paragraphs — Arguments with Evidence ▼

Each argument paragraph follows this pattern: topic sentence → explanation → evidence or example → link back to thesis. Use these academic phrases: כל פסקת טיעון עוקבת אחרי דפוס זה: משפט נושא → הסבר → ראיה או דוגמה → חיבור לתזה. השתמשו בביטויים אקדמיים:

First argument טיעון ראשון

First and foremost, it is important to state that…
To begin with, it is worth mentioning that…
Then: Research has shown… / Studies have proved… ראשית — עדות מחקרית

Second argument טיעון שני

Another point that should be taken into consideration is (that)…
A further argument in favour of / against… is…
Then: For example… / For instance… טיעון נוסף — דוגמה

Counter-argument טיעון נגד

Opponents / Proponents of … take a different view about…
One commonly heard objection to this argument is…
Then refute: However… / Nevertheless… / Despite this… הכירו בצד השני — ואז הפריכו
✅ Two Body Paragraphs
First and foremost, it is important to state that constant connectivity is eroding our capacity for deep, focused attention. Research has shown that the average person checks their phone over 150 times a day — a habit that fragments concentration and makes sustained conversation increasingly difficult. When we sit with a friend yet compulsively glance at a screen, we signal, however unintentionally, that the digital world takes priority over the person in front of us.

Another point that should be taken into consideration is the growing body of evidence linking heavy social media use to feelings of loneliness and inadequacy. Studies have proved that teenagers who spend more than three hours a day on social platforms report significantly higher levels of anxiety. For instance, the curated nature of online profiles creates a constant, unfair comparison between one's private reality and another's public performance.
🍞 Conclusion — Restate, Reinforce, Remember ▼

Your conclusion should do three things: summarise, restate your position (in new words), and end with a memorable final thought. Use: המסקנה צריכה לעשות שלושה דברים: לסכם, לחזור על עמדתכם (במילים חדשות), ולסיים במחשבה בלתי נשכחת:

To sum up, as I have mentioned above, I am totally convinced that... In conclusion, never have I been more convinced that... All things considered, I strongly believe that... To conclude, it is essential to remember that... It is about time people realized / understood that... If I were ... I would... Considering everything I have just written, you too will agree that...
✅ Strong Concluding Paragraph
To sum up, as I have mentioned above, I am totally convinced that while social media can serve as a valuable tool for communication, its uncritical and excessive use poses a genuine threat to the quality of our interpersonal relationships. Opponents of this view may argue that technology simply reflects human choice — and that is a fair point. Nevertheless, the freedom to choose comes with the responsibility to choose wisely. It is about time we recognized that a like on a screen is no substitute for genuine human presence, and that protecting our capacity for real connection may be one of the most important choices of our generation.
Compare these arguments
❌ Weak Argument
I think social media is bad. People use it too much. It makes them sad. Also they don't talk to friends. I think it should be banned. That is my opinion.

No connectors, no evidence, no counter-argument, no conclusion אין מחברים, אין ראיות, אין טיעון נגד, אין מסקנה
✅ Strong Argument
Although many people believe social media connects us, it is, in my opinion, a misconception. First and foremost, research has shown that heavy use is linked to loneliness. Another point worth considering is that constant scrolling destroys our ability to focus. Opponents may argue that this depends on the user — however, the evidence suggests otherwise. To conclude, I am totally convinced that we must use social media with far greater awareness.

Academic openers, evidence, counter-argument, strong conclusion פתיחות אקדמיות, ראיות, טיעון נגד, מסקנה חזקה
